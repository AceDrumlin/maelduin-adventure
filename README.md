# The Voyage of Mael Duin — A Classic Text Adventure

*Based on the Irish folk epic "Imram Mael Duin" (The Voyage of Mael Dúin)*

Set sail across a mythical Atlantic, visit strange and wondrous islands, talk to a judgmental cat, out-laugh a laughing king, and discover that the real treasure was the friends you made along the way. Or the golden apple. One of those.

## Quick Start

### Console Edition
```bash
python3 console.py
```

### Web Edition
```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 web/app.py
# Open http://localhost:5000
```

## About the Story

*The Voyage of Mael Duin* is a medieval Irish immram (voyage tale) found in the 11th-century *Book of the Dun Cow*. It tells of Mael Duin, a warrior who sets out to avenge his father's murder and instead finds himself on an epic journey across 31+ magical islands — each stranger than the last.

The story is famous for:
- Giant ants that farm golden fruit
- A cat that judges trespassers
- An island where everyone laughs endlessly
- A glass bridge to a crystal palace
- A prophetic boy who reads a book with no pages
- And the most important lesson: vengeance empties the drinker

## Game Features

- **30+ islands** to discover, each with unique puzzles and encounters
- **Full crew system** — your poet, champion, and navigator each have personalities
- **Humor & absurdity** — the original story is delightfully weird, and so is the game
- **Multiple solutions** — talk your way out, fight, bribe, or just run away
- **Web & console** — play in your terminal or in a browser
